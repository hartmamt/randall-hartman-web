“The lightning had not stayed inside the picture. We had learned to aim it at people.”from A God Awful God

I spent forty years explaining God. Now I write about what I got wrong.

For most of my life I was afraid of God. I was seven when I opened our family Bible and saw Him scowling from the clouds. I learned He was angry, watching, keeping score.

Somewhere along the way the picture began to change, and so did the way I saw the people my church taught me to fear. This is where I write about that change, honestly and without easy answers.
